# Emiliano Ruiz Gomez

I’m an applied AI product engineer in Montréal. I build local-first software, agent systems with real boundaries, evaluation harnesses, and practical business automation.

My route into engineering started in operations, marketing, and client-facing work. I learned to see the messy human problem first: the missed handoff, the confusing interface, the campaign that could not be repeated, or the AI demo nobody could safely depend on. Software became the way I turn those problems into bounded, testable products.

## What I’m building

### Echo — private pre-release product

A clean Swift/macOS voice workspace for private dictation, meetings, evidence-grounded notes, and structured memory. I own the product direction, clean architecture, local-model integration, evaluation, delivery reliability, and release engineering.

Echo uses a local Parakeet Core ML speech route, local Qwen meeting intelligence, deterministic vocabulary correction, model routing, challenger evaluation, and benchmark promotion gates. It is an enhanced local-AI product pipeline—not a claim of newly trained foundation-model weights.

[Read the case study](https://emilianoontherunn.github.io/projects/echo) · [View the dedicated showcase](https://echo-local-intelligence.pages.dev/)

### Agent Launch Lab — private business project

A runtime-independent control plane for mission contracts, permissions, artifact validation, and audit evidence. Its reference workflow cannot pass until declared evidence and report artifacts satisfy explicit gates.

[Read the case study](https://emilianoontherunn.github.io/projects/agent-launch)

### Ad Video Engine — open source

A brief-driven Python engine that converts marketing intent into reviewable campaign artifacts. It validates the brief, applies a production-quality structured prompt, passes concepts through a private ranking boundary, then produces stable ad specifications, posting copy, QA, and a manifest.

[Source](https://github.com/EmilianoOnTheRunn/ad-video-engine) · [Case study](https://emilianoontherunn.github.io/projects/ad-video-engine)

### CreatorStack Systems — open source and attributed adaptation

A fixture-testable lead research CLI with a private qualification-policy interface, plus an explicitly attributed evaluation fork of Vercel Labs Open Agents. CreatorStack's production workflow skills and operating playbooks remain private.

[Lead agent](https://github.com/EmilianoOnTheRunn/creatorstack-lead-agent) · [Cloud agents](https://github.com/EmilianoOnTheRunn/creatorstack-cloud-agents) · [Case study](https://emilianoontherunn.github.io/projects/creatorstack)

## How I work

- Start with the user, the decision, and the cost of failure.
- Separate model behavior from product safeguards and delivery behavior.
- Make permissions, artifacts, review points, and “done” explicit.
- Keep the protocol and limitations beside every benchmark.
- Ship the whole path: architecture, tests, packaging, permissions, recovery, and documentation.
